Every week TikTok seems to be doing something new: a dance, a sound, a format, a challenge. Trends are what make the platform feel alive, and they are also a fast way for creators to be seen. This guide explains how trends work, how to use them without losing your own voice and how to save the TikTok videos you want to keep.

## What a TikTok trend is

A trend is a shared idea that many people remake in their own way. It can be:

- **A sound,** where creators use the same audio clip with different visuals.
- **A format,** such as "day in the life", "get ready with me" or a specific editing style.
- **A challenge,** where people attempt the same task and share the result.
- **A phrase or meme,** reused in captions or on screen.

Trends spread because they are easy to copy, and each version makes the next one more likely to be seen.

## How to find trends early

Being early helps, since a trend is most useful before everyone has done it. You can:

1. **Watch your For You page** for repeated sounds and formats.
2. **Check the sounds page,** where popular audio is listed with the number of videos.
3. **Follow creators in your niche** and see what they are trying.
4. **Search hashtags and topics** you care about.

Not every trend suits you, and that is fine. Pick the ones that match your niche and your style.

## How to join a trend without copying

The best trend videos add something of their own:

- **Keep the format, change the subject.** Bring the trend into your field: cooking, fitness, small business, travel.
- **Open strong.** Get to the point in the first second.
- **Add text on screen** so the idea works without sound.
- **Be quick.** Post while the trend is still fresh.
- **Stay authentic.** A trend that does not fit you tends to look forced.

A trend that has nothing to do with your niche can bring views without followers, so choose carefully. For more on growing an audience, read our [tips to grow your social media following](/blog/grow-social-media-following-2026).

## Music and copyright

Sounds in TikTok's library are licensed for use in the app, and the rules can differ for business accounts and for videos you post elsewhere. If you take a video with a popular song to another platform, the music may not be cleared there. Check the rules of each platform and use royalty-free audio for anything you plan to publish outside TikTok.

## Saving videos you love

## Your own TikToks

If you made the video, the best copy is the original from your phone or editor, without a watermark. If you only have the published version, saving it is a reasonable backup, and you can reuse it on other platforms. A version with someone else's watermark tends to look recycled elsewhere, so post a clean export whenever you can.

## Reusing other people's videos

Saving a video to watch offline is one thing. Posting it again, using it in a compilation or putting it in an ad without permission is another, and it can lead to takedowns.
