Threads is now full of short videos: quick tips, clips from events, funny moments and small tutorials. But like most social platforms, it gives you a way to like and repost a video, and no clear way to keep it. This guide shows how to save a public Threads video as a file, what to expect and how to reuse videos fairly.

## Why saving a Threads video is not obvious

Threads is built around viewing and sharing inside the app. A video is meant to stay on the platform, so there is no simple "download" button for other people's posts. That is fine for casual browsing, but not when you want to watch offline, keep a backup of your own clip or use it in a project you have permission for.

## Saving a public Threads video

For a public video, a browser tool can save it as an MP4. Here is how:

1. Open the Threads post that contains the video.
2. Tap the share icon, then **Copy link**.
3. Open our [Threads video downloader](/threads-video-downloader).
4. Paste the link. The Threads tab is selected for you.
5. Check the preview and choose **Download**.

Both threads.net and threads.com links work. You do not need to log in, and you should never enter your Threads or Instagram password into a downloader.

## What works, and what does not

**Works:** videos in public posts.

**Does not work:** posts from private profiles, posts that have been deleted, and posts with only text or images. If a post has no video, the tool will say so instead of returning something unrelated.

If a link fails, open it in a private browser window while logged out. If you cannot see the post there, a downloader cannot either.

## Quality and sound

You get the video in a standard format that plays on most devices. Quality depends on what Threads serves for that post, so a saved copy will not be sharper than the original. For your own videos, the best version is always the file you recorded, not the one uploaded to any platform.

## Your own videos come first

If you posted the video, keep the source file. Export from your editor in MP4, name it clearly and keep it in cloud storage. That protects you if a post is removed or an account is lost. Downloading your own published copy is a reasonable backup when you no longer have the original.

## Reusing videos fairly

A video on Threads belongs to its creator, even when it is public. That leads to a few simple rules:

- **Watching privately is fine.** Saving a clip to watch offline is a common, low-risk use.
- **Ask before you repost.** Reposting someone's video on another platform, in a video of your own or in a paid project needs their permission.
- **Credit the creator.** Name them and link to the original when they agree.
- **Remove it on request.** If the creator asks, take it down.

## Troubleshooting

- **"No video found":** the post has only text or images. Copy the link from a post with a video.
- **"Private or login required":** the profile is private.
- **A very new post:** wait a minute for it to finish processing.
- **A message about limits:** try again shortly.

## Tips for creators who post video on Threads

Keep clips short and clear, with captions so viewers can follow without sound. Put your point in the first second, and ask a question in your text to invite replies. If a video performs well, adapt it for other platforms, and keep your original so you can export it in the right shape for each.
